GOLD 2025 Changemaker Program · Ministry of Education, Taiwan

A riverside trail that helps seniors walk again.

We turned a 2.8 km trail along the Keelung River in Nuannuan, an aging neighborhood of Keelung, into a guided walking route with local stories and safe exercises for older residents.

The problem

The trail was there. Older residents had stopped using it.

Fear of falling

About 19% of Nuannuan's residents are over 65. Keelung's wet winters make paths slippery, and after one fall many seniors stop going out, which speeds up physical decline.

No guidance on the trail

The trail had no safety information or walking guidance, so residents with weaker balance or strength didn't know which parts were safe for them.

Lost local memory

New housing brought in new residents who didn't know the river's history, while the older residents who did were walking it less. The two groups rarely met.

What we did · Apr – Nov 2025

From interviews to a map people actually use

  1. Listened first

    Interviewed 23 people on the trail and in the community: trail users at different times of day, neighborhood chiefs, and community association leaders. We learned when and how people used the trail, and collected local stories about the old coal railway, the military housing villages, and the riverside Earth God temple.

  2. Assessed the trail like a clinician

    Our physical therapist measured the whole trail: 6 entrances, 5 staircases, 4 railings and 3 benches. We analyzed slopes, uneven surfaces and rest points, then graded the routes by difficulty.

  3. Designed exercises into the trail

    We created 4 exercises that use the trail's existing railings and stairs: stretching, balance, coordination and upper-body strength. Each one has its own spot on the map.

  4. Built the map with residents

    We combined stories, routes and exercises into one illustrated map with QR codes linking to 4 video tutorials. Residents and partners reviewed every draft, and we went through more than 20 versions.

  5. Walked it together

    We hosted 2 guided “Rejuvenation Walk” events in July 2025 (25 and 15 participants), mixing story-telling, exercises on the trail, and a playful “Rejuvenation Dance” at the Earth God temple.

  6. Left it in place

    We printed and handed out 60 maps, installed a map signboard on the trail, and set up LINE, Instagram and YouTube channels so residents can keep using it after the project ended.

What we learned

Our first poster looked good to us. Residents didn't understand it.

The first event poster got praise from friends for its design. Once we put it up in the neighborhood, several older residents asked us, “What is this event actually about?”

We redesigned it with community members' advice: bigger type, clearer images, and plainer wording. After the new poster went up, both events filled up quickly. We applied the same lesson to the map, revising it through more than 20 versions until residents could read it easily.

Results

What changed

For residents

All surveyed participants were satisfied or very satisfied. Participants said they learned how to use the trail safely, and new and long-time residents met through the walks.

Residents took part themselves

One participant filmed the walk with his own drone and edited two videos for YouTube. He told us he records moments like this so he can look back on them later.

Funding and partners

We ran the project on a budget of NT$209,410: a NT$100,000 government grant, plus NT$109,410 the team raised from other sources. We partnered with 6 local organizations.

Now · 2026 · In progress

Ask the Earth God: a health app for the temple on the trail

Many older residents already walk to the Fu'an Temple by the suspension bridge every morning. In 2026 we are building on that habit with a mobile web app, voiced by the temple's Earth God, that turns the daily walk into a health routine.

  • Step counter with three walking routes (1K, 2K and 3K) and safety notes for each trail entrance and staircase
  • A 10-question health quiz in plain, friendly language
  • Health Q&A with the Earth God, plus a daily blessing
  • Video lessons for our “Rejuvenation Dance”
